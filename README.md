Welcome to the ECE Notes project!  
This repository contains curated notes, resources, and study materials for the **Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE)** program (Batch 2022–2026).

The materials are mainly designed for students under the **2019 Regulation**. Students under the **2023 Regulation** may find some subjects different.

# 💫 Motivation

Before I switched to on-campus courses, I took all of my classes online and hardly interacted with any upperclassmen.

Without assistance, it was difficult for me to locate relevant study materials and organize my preparation effectively.

This repository was created to help students easily access useful academic resources and guidance.
